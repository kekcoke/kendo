- **EventsController** — 5 JWT-authenticated routes: GET list (paginated, owner-scoped), GET by id (owner only), POST create, PATCH update (owner only), DELETE hard delete

- **Docker compose + .env.example** — KENDO__EVENT_EMBEDDING__DIM=1024, KENDO__EVENT_EMBEDDING__MODEL=BGE-large-en-v1.5
